Private AI for defined business tasks.

ViVeSec AI Box combines local computing with proprietary agent software. Prepare reports, summaries and decision memos from protected company documents, within existing access rights.

Product renderings are illustrative, not configuration-specific technical drawings.

Give the agent a defined task.

Define the output you need and the relevant sources. Agent-based workflows guide document-based tasks through to a draft you can review, with citations to check against the originals. The AI Box offering includes one year of support and ongoing development.

  • Defined tasks over selected company sources
  • Permissions are not widened by a question
  • Output remains a review aid

Put company documents to work.

Give the agent a defined task using relevant company documents. Prepare summaries, reports, decision memos and presentations without widening anyone’s document permissions.

Document search

Ask questions across the drives you can access. Open the cited passages and original documents to check the answer.

Meeting summaries

Turn meeting material into a summary of decisions, documented action items, owners and deadlines.

Reports and project tracking

Prepare status reports and review documented tasks, milestones and risks from your source files.

Decision memos and presentations

Compare documented alternatives, draft a decision memo or prepare a presentation outline for human review.
